## 2. Pricing and the Reasoning

| Plan | Price | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Founding Member | $97/month, locked for life | The first wave on the waitlist. Full MŌNAI for one workspace. |
| Standard | $149/month | Everyone after the founding window closes. Same product. |
| Business Tier | $299/month | Teams. Multiple seats, manager approvals, team-level audit view. |
| Done-For-You Setup | $500 one time, optional | We connect everything, load the priorities, and walk through the first brief. |

**Why these numbers.** The founding price rewards the people who trust you early, and "locked for life" gives them a reason to act now instead of later. Standard at $149 is still far below one day a month of a part-time assistant, so the value is obvious to a solo owner. Business at $299 covers the real cost of supporting a team with approvals and seats, and it anchors Standard as the sensible middle. The $500 setup is for owners who want zero friction, and it pays for the hands-on hour that turns a signup into a daily habit.

**Decisions still open**

- How many founding seats, and for how long. Suggested: 100 seats or 90 days, whichever comes first. Scarcity only works if the window actually closes.
- Annual billing discount. Suggested: two months free on annual (Standard $1,490/yr, Business $2,990/yr).
- Nonprofit and ministry rate. Suggested: Standard features at the founding price, verified by EIN, no time limit.

## 3. Three-Year Planning Scenarios

These are planning scenarios, not guarantees. They use a blended $125 per month per workspace (a mix of Founding, Standard, and Business plans) and assume a share of new workspaces choose the $500 setup.

Annual recurring revenue = average workspaces × $125 × 12 months.

### Conservative
Slow, steady word of mouth. 30% of new workspaces buy setup.

| Year | Average workspaces | Annual recurring revenue | Setup fee revenue | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 25 | $37,500 | $6,000 | $43,500 |
| Year 2 | 60 | $90,000 | $9,000 | $99,000 |
| Year 3 | 120 | $180,000 | $15,000 | $195,000 |

### Moderate
Consistent marketing and referrals from happy members. 35% buy setup.

| Year | Average workspaces | Annual recurring revenue | Setup fee revenue | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 60 | $90,000 | $17,500 | $107,500 |
| Year 2 | 180 | $270,000 | $35,000 | $305,000 |
| Year 3 | 400 | $600,000 | $60,000 | $660,000 |

### Optimistic
Strong launch plus ministry and association partnerships. 40% buy setup.

| Year | Average workspaces | Annual recurring revenue | Setup fee revenue | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 120 | $180,000 | $40,000 | $220,000 |
| Year 2 | 400 | $600,000 | $90,000 | $690,000 |
| Year 3 | 1,000 | $1,500,000 | $180,000 | $1,680,000 |

**What moves the numbers.** Two things more than anything else: how many founding members sign in the first ninety days, and how many of them stay. At a blended $125, every 10 workspaces kept for a year is $15,000. Keep monthly churn under 3% and every scenario climbs.

**Costs are not in these tables.** Before using any scenario for a decision, subtract hosting and model usage per workspace, payment processing (about 3%), support time, and marketing spend.
